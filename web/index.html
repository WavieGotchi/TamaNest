TAMANEST

Colour Tamagotchi emulator for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.16.

This browser cannot install firmware. Please use Chrome or Edge on a computer, or Chrome on an Android phone or tablet. Installing needs a secure page (https).

Using a phone or tablet and your device is not in the list? Try alternate USB mode.

HOW TO INSTALL
  1. Connect your device to this computer, or to an Android phone or tablet, with a USB-C data cable (or a USB-C adapter).
  2. Press INSTALL / UPDATE and choose your device from the list (it will show as "USB JTAG/serial debug unit"; other kinds of device are not listed).
  3. First install: select erase.
  4. Updating later: do NOT select erase - your settings, saves, and your ROMs will not be erased.
  5. Wait until it says done; your device will restart into TamaNest.

If your device is not in the list, hold its BOOT key (left key when the buttons are on top) while plugging it in, then try again.

YOUR ROMS

TamaNest does not include any game ROMs. Dump the ROMs of toys you own and copy your .bin files to the SD card's roms folder (TamaNest's USB DRIVE mode can be used to mount the SD on your computer). Use an 8-32 GB card formatted as FAT32, larger and fancier cards may reduce battery life. Smaller and simpler is best.

UPDATES WITHOUT THIS PAGE

On your device: SETTINGS > UPDATE > CHECK ONLINE downloads and installs a new version over Wi-Fi (choose your network on the device; the password is typed on the touch screen).

Without Wi-Fi: copy tamanest.bin from the latest release to the SD card's update folder, then choose SETTINGS > UPDATE > SD CARD.
